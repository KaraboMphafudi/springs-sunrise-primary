About Us

Founded in 1995, Springs Sunrise Primary is dedicated to nurturing young minds with a balanced curriculum of academics, sport, and culture.

Mission: To empower every learner to reach their full potential in a safe, supportive environment.

Vision: Building responsible, confident citizens for tomorrow's South Africa.

"Every child is a star. We just help them shine."
– Mrs. Dlamini, Principal
